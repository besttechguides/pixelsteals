How to use a deal list like this

  • Check the region. Keys from some stores are region-locked. All the stores tracked here sell to UK customers, but read the store page for any "not available in your region" note.
  • Same game, several stores? Pick the store that delivers to the platform you use. A GOG copy is DRM-free and installs through GOG Galaxy or a plain installer; most other stores give you a Steam key.
  • Look at what's included. "Complete", "GOTY" and "Definitive" editions often bundle DLC that's sold separately elsewhere.
  • Look up the price history. A big percentage off isn't always the lowest price ever. CheapShark and IsThereAnyDeal both show price history so you can see whether it's worth waiting.
  • Know your refund rights. Steam offers refunds within 14 days of purchase if you've played for under two hours. Other stores have their own policies; under UK law your right to cancel a digital download can end once you start downloading, if you agreed to that at checkout.
