About PixelSteals

PixelSteals is a small, independent UK site for people who love games and tech but don't love paying full price. We track free games and well-reviewed PC deals every day and publish them here and in a free daily email.

How it works

Deal data comes from public sources: the Epic Games Store's free-games feed and CheapShark, a price-comparison service covering legitimate digital stores. Our pages update automatically each morning, with the time of the last update shown on every data page. Our guides are written for UK players, with prices taken from official sources and dated so you know how fresh they are.

What we won't do

  • List grey-market key resellers or anything that infringes copyright.
  • Let commissions decide which deals appear. Deals are chosen by discount, rating and price.
  • Sell or share your email address.

How we're funded

PixelSteals is free. We may earn money from advertising and from affiliate links, which are always disclosed. See our affiliate disclosure and privacy policy.
